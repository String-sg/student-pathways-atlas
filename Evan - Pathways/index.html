🎯 SEC 2028 Minimum Entry Requirements (MER)

Adapted for Secondary 2 students based on MOE's official SEC 2028 admissions framework. Eligibility shows whether general admission criteria are met. Specific courses also require course-specific prerequisites and cut-off points.

* Official MOE 2028 PSE Admissions Framework Notes:
• Pre-U (JC & MI L1R4): All 5 subjects in L1R4 must be G3. G1 MTL (Grade A–D) satisfies the subject prerequisite for JC/MI (students take MTL 'B'), but G1 MTL is excluded from L1R4, ELR2B2, and PFP aggregate scoring. Max 3 ordinary bonus points (CCA + HMTL + Affiliation) plus 2 LEP points.
• Polytechnic (ELR2B2): Net score ≤ 22 (Diploma in Nursing ≤ 24). EL, R1, R2, and B1 require G3 grades; B2 can be taken at G2 or G3 (Grade 1–4 equivalent). G3 Grade 9 and G2 Grade 5 & 6 cannot be used.
• Polytechnic Foundation Programme (PFP): Gross ELMAB3 ≤ 12. G3 Grade 9 and G2 Grade 5 & 6 cannot be used. EL & Math must be Grade 1–3, with 1 relevant B3 subject Grade 1–3 and other B3 Grade 1–4.
• ITE Higher Nitec (2-Year vs 3-Year): 2-Year direct entry requires gross ELMAB3 ≤ 19 (G3 9 and G2 6 can be used for aggregate). 3-Year uses official 1–5 scale (G3 A1–D7 / G2 1–3 / G1 A = 1 pt; G3 E8 / G2 4 / G1 B = 2 pts; G3 9 / G2 5 / G1 C = 3 pts; G2 6 / G1 D = 4 pts; G1 E = 5 pts).
• 5th Year Secondary (Sec 5): Eligible if meeting Criterion 1 (≥3 G3 Passes and not qualifying for JC/MI/Poly) OR Criterion 2 (≤2 G3 Passes & Gross ELMAB3 ≤ 21, ELB3 ≤ 14, or MAB3 ≤ 14 at G2). Gross ELMAB3 ≤ 19 enables offering all subjects at G3 in Sec 5.

🩺 Nursing Career Pathway: Enrolled Nurse to Registered Nurse & Degree

If a student qualifies for the 3-Year Higher Nitec pathway, Singapore offers a structured, progressive career ladder from an Enrolled Nurse (EN) up to a Registered Nurse (RN) and a University Degree. No student is locked out of a nursing career!

Stage 1 • Duration: 3 Years

ITE 3-Year Higher Nitec in Nursing

Campus: ITE College East (School of Health Sciences)

  • Year 1: Broad-based foundational healthcare and biological science modules.
  • Years 2 & 3: Core nursing specialization, high-tech simulation ward practice, and extensive hospital postings.
  • Outcome: Graduate with Higher Nitec in Nursing; eligible to register with Singapore Nursing Board (SNB) to practise as a licensed Enrolled Nurse (EN).
Stage 2 • Duration: 2 to 3 Years

Diploma in Nursing → Registered Nurse (RN)

Two distinct pathways to upgrade to a Registered Nurse (RN):

  • Route A: Full-time Polytechnic Diploma at NYP or NP (2 to 3 years). With GPA ≥ 3.0, graduates qualify for advanced placement into an accelerated 2-year or 2.5-year route.
  • Route B: ITE Work-Study Diploma (WSDip) in Nursing (3 years). Work as an Enrolled Nurse earning full salary while fully sponsored for part-time diploma training!
Stage 3 • Duration: 2 to 3 Years

University Degree in Nursing (Optional)

Institutions: National University of Singapore (NUS) or Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT).

  • Programme: Bachelor of Science in Nursing.
  • Duration: 2 to 3 years (diploma graduates receive credit exemptions).
  • Outcome: Direct progression to Senior Staff Nurse, Nurse Clinician, Nurse Educator, or Advanced Practice Nurse (APN).

Glossary of Terms (MOE 2028 Admissions Framework)

SEC
Singapore-Cambridge Secondary Education Certificate (taken at the end of Secondary 4 from 2027/2028 under Full SBB).
G1, G2, G3
General 1, 2, and 3 under Full SBB. G3 is Express standard, G2 is N(A) standard, and G1 is N(T) standard.
L1R4 (JC & MI)
All 5 subjects must be taken at G3 level. Gross L1R4 ≤ 16 for JC, ≤ 20 for MI. G3 English A1–C6 and Math A1–D7 compulsory. Max 3 ordinary bonus points (CCA + HMTL + Affiliation) plus 2 LEP bonus points.
ELR2B2 (Poly Year 1)
English + 2 Relevant + Best 2 other subjects. EL, R1, R2, B1 use G3 grades; B2 can be G2 or mapped from G3 (grades 1–4). Net score ≤ 22 (Nursing ≤ 24) after CCA deduction. G3 grade 9 and G2 grades 5 & 6 cannot be used.
ELMAB3 (PFP & ITE 2-Yr)
English + Mathematics + Best 3 other subjects using G2 equivalent grades. PFP requires gross ELMAB3 ≤ 12 (grades 5 & 6 barred). 2-Year Higher Nitec requires gross ELMAB3 ≤ 19 (G3 9 mapped to 5 and G2 6 can be used for aggregate).
ITE 3-Year Point Scale
Official MOE conversion: G3 A1–D7 / G2 1–3 / G1 A = 1 pt; G3 E8 / G2 4 / G1 B = 2 pts; G3 9 / G2 5 / G1 C = 3 pts; G2 6 / G1 D = 4 pts; G1 E = 5 pts. CCA Bonus: 1 or 2 pts deducted for net aggregate score.
5th Year Secondary (Sec 5)
Eligible under MOE Full SBB if meeting Criterion 1 (≥3 G3 passes, not qualifying for JC/MI/Poly) OR Criterion 2 (≤2 G3 passes with gross ELMAB3 ≤ 21, ELB3 ≤ 14, or MAB3 ≤ 14 at G2). Gross ELMAB3 ≤ 19 allows offering all subjects at G3 in Sec 5.
G1 Mother Tongue
Grade A to D satisfies the subject entry requirement for JC and MI (students will offer MTL 'B'). It cannot be computed into L1R4, ELR2B2, or PFP aggregates, but counts 1–4 points for ITE 3-Year Higher Nitec.
MTL 'B'
A pre-university Mother Tongue syllabus at JC and MI with greater emphasis on practical listening and spoken skills, offered to students who took G1 Mother Tongue in secondary school.
Gross vs Net
JC (≤16), MI (≤20), PFP (≤12), and ITE 2-Year (≤19) use gross scores for admission eligibility. Poly Year 1 (≤22) uses net aggregate after deducting up to 2 CCA bonus points.
HMTL
Higher Mother Tongue Language (G3). Passing both G3 English and HMTL awards 2 bonus points for JC admission when English is L1 (capped at 3 total bonus points).
Science at our school
Our school offers Double Science (split into 2: Chemistry, Physics, Biology) and Combined Science. Triple Science is not offered.
Computing & Business
Our school offers Computing across G1, G2, and G3 levels. Computing directly qualifies as a relevant subject for Polytechnic Information & Digital Technologies diplomas (ELR2B2-C), ITE Infocomm courses (R1B3-B), and counts towards JC/MI L1R4. Business (EBS) is offered at G1 level.
Enrolled Nurse (EN)
A licensed healthcare professional registered with the Singapore Nursing Board (SNB) via the ITE 3-Year Higher Nitec in Nursing (College East). ENs deliver vital patient care and can upgrade to Registered Nurse via Polytechnic Diploma or ITE Work-Study Diploma.
Registered Nurse (RN)
A professional nurse registered with SNB after completing a Polytechnic Diploma in Nursing, ITE Work-Study Diploma, or University Degree.
Work-Study Diploma (Nursing)
A 3-year fully sponsored upgrading diploma by ITE in partnership with public healthcare clusters. Enrolled Nurses work full-time earning a regular monthly salary while completing part-time diploma training to upgrade to Registered Nurses.
